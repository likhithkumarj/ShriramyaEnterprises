Moringa (Moringa Oleifera)

Harvested from certified plantations across south India, our Moringa is shade-dried or gently dehydrated to preserve chlorophyll, antioxidants, essential amino acids, and high plant-based protein content. Available in fine powders, leaf cuts, and seed lots.

Request bulk quotation View specifications

Available forms

Supplied in grades tested for purity, microbial safety, and active nutrient density.

Moringa Leaf Powder

Shade dried, micro-pulverized (80–100 mesh)

Superfine green powder milled from thoroughly washed, stem-free moringa leaves. Retains its deep chlorophyll-green color and fresh botanical aroma.

  • Vibrant natural deep green color (non-irradiated)
  • Contains 25% – 30% complete plant protein
  • Readily dispersible in smoothies, capsules, and green blends

Dehydrated Moringa Leaves

Whole leaves & tea bag cut (TBC)

Cleaned, sorted, and gently dried whole leaves free from twigs and dust. Popular among herbal tea blenders and dietary decoction manufacturers.

  • Uniform tea-cut or whole leaf options
  • Bright green appearance with intact leaf structure
  • Excellent infusion clarity and delicate herbal profile

Moringa Seeds (PKM-1 / Wild)

Machine cleaned & hand-graded seeds

Clean, winged or de-winged moringa seeds carefully harvested for high oil yield, cold-press oil extraction, water clarification, and agricultural sowing.

  • High oil content (35% – 40% ben oil)
  • Sortex clean quality with high germination rates
  • Available in winged and kernel-separated forms

Common applications

Nutraceuticals & Supplements
