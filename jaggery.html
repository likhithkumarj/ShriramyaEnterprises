Organic Jaggery

Available forms

Organic Jaggery Powder

Free-flowing & easily soluble

Micro-pulverized and dried to optimal moisture levels to resist caking. Melts smoothly in beverages, confectionery batters, and health food mixes.

  • Natural golden to rich amber tone
  • Uniform mesh consistency for industrial blenders
  • No chemical bleaches, preservatives, or anti-caking additives

Solid Jaggery Lumps & Blocks

Traditional round shapes & square blocks

Dense, aromatic solid jaggery molded in standard weight sizes. Highly sought after for traditional food production, exports, and bulk retail packaging.

  • Available in 500g, 1kg, 5kg, and 10kg blocks
  • Compact structure with extended shelf stability
  • Rich molasses notes and authentic caramel aroma
