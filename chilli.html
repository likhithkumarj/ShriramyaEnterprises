Whole, Flakes & Ground Chilli

Green and Red Chilli

From high-color Byadgi varieties to intense heat Guntur and Teja chillies, we source directly from major growing regions across India. Available in whole pods (stemmed or stemless), customized flakes, and fine-ground powders.

Request bulk quotation View specifications

Available forms

Supplied across distinct heat levels (SHU) and color ratings (ASTA) to match exact formula needs.

Chilli Flakes (Crushed Chilli)

Standard cut or custom coarse mesh

Uniformly cut flakes with balanced seed-to-flake ratio. Perfect for pizza toppings, seasoning rubs, and ready-meal garnishes.

  • Controlled seed percentage (standard or seedless)
  • Vibrant bright red visual appearance
  • Available in low to high heat profiles

Whole Chilli (With Stem)

Direct sun-dried farmer lots

Carefully hand-graded whole dried red chillies retaining the stem. Preserves essential oils during prolonged storage.

  • Varieties: Guntur, Teja (S17), Byadgi, 334
  • Free from fungal discoloration and broken pods
  • Natural sheen and firm pod structure

Stemless Whole Chilli

100% de-stemmed for direct grinding

Hand-stemmed or mechanically clipped to eliminate stem weight, saving processing time and grinding costs for spice manufacturers.

  • Stemless purity above 99%
  • Eliminates debris and bitterness from stems
  • Directly feedable into extraction and milling lines

Premium Red Chilli Powder

Heat-controlled micro-milling

Finely pulverized red chilli milled at controlled low temperatures to retain color pigments and volatile oleoresins.

  • Color values from 60 to 140+ ASTA
  • Heat ratings from 20,000 up to 85,000 SHU
  • Guaranteed free from Sudan dyes or synthetic colors

Dehydrated Green Chilli Powder

Vibrant color & fresh chili punch

Manufactured from freshly harvested green chillies through low-temperature dehydration. Imparts a sharp, zesty vegetal pungency without adding moisture.

  • Preserves the signature fresh green aroma
  • Ideal for instant snacks, marinades, and dry rubs
  • Uniform fine mesh for easy dispersion

Specifications & quality standards

Stringent quality control on color, capsaicin content, moisture, and microbiology.

Common applications

Spice Seasoning Blends

Key component of curry powders, barbecue rubs, ramen seasonings, and taco blends.

Sauces, Salsas & Pickles

Adds intense natural red color, piquancy, and shelf stability to hot sauces and condiments.

Snack Food Extrusion

Dehydrated green and red chilli powders provide dusting flavor for chips, puffs, and nuts.

Oleoresin Extraction

High capsaicin lots supplied for industrial solvent and supercritical CO2 extraction.
