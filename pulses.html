Whole Grains & Split Dals

Agricultural Pulses & Lentils

Sourced from fertile pulse farming belts across India, our pulses undergo rigorous optical color sorting, grading, and destoning. We supply unpolished, naturally nutritious whole and split dals to food processors, institutional distributors, and packers.

Request bulk quotation View specifications

Varieties & grades in stock

Available in whole grain, split with skin, or unpolished washed dals.

Toor Dal (Pigeon Pea)

Unpolished fatka & desi varieties

Naturally processed without artificial water polishing, leather buffing, or mineral oils. Boils quickly with rich aroma and thick culinary gravy texture.

  • Desi and hybrid premium varieties
  • Uniform grain size with minimal broken grains
  • High protein content and authentic flavour

Moong (Green Gram & Yellow Dal)

Whole green, split chilka & washed yellow

Triple-cleaned moong beans ideal for sprouting, packaged grocery retail, or milling into yellow split moong dal.

  • Whole green shiny and medium bold grains
  • Yellow moong dal unpolished with zero artificial luster
  • Rapid cooking and easy digestibility

Urad (Black Gram)

Whole black matpe & washed white urad

Sortex graded black gram essential for fermentation batters, papad manufacturers, and traditional culinary delicacies.

  • High mucilage / foaming capacity for fermentation
  • Washed white urad whole (Gota) and split options
  • Free from live infestation and debris

Chana Dal & Chickpeas

Desi chana, chana dal & Kabuli chickpeas

Carefully calibrated for size and moisture. Milled into sweet, golden chana dal or used in commercial snacks and besan milling.

  • Consistent bold sizes (75/80, 42/44 count for Kabuli)
  • Prime chana dal for besan flour mills and namkeens
  • Low moisture guaranteeing extended shelf stability

Masoor (Red Lentils)

Whole brown masoor & split red lentils (Football)

Machine cleaned and sortex graded whole brown lentils and split red lentils for soups, canned pulses, and exports.

  • Uniform bright orange-red color
  • Quick-cooking with smooth velvety texture
  • Strict control on foreign seeds and stones

Common applications

Flour & Besan Milling

Supplied directly to industrial millers producing gram flour, pulse protein isolates, and lentil flours.

Snack & Namkeen Frying

Uniformly calibrated split dals utilized for frying spiced snacks, bhujia, roasted dal, and papads.

Institutional & Horeca

Consistent cooking times and high yield make them the preferred choice for industrial caterers.

Retail Repackaging

Sortex clean purity ensures zero manual sorting needed before packaging into consumer pouches.
