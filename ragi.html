Available forms

Raw Whole Finger Millet

Machine cleaned & Sortex graded

Unprocessed whole grains free from foreign matter, stones, and weed seeds. Ideal for commercial milling, malting, and processing units.

  • Sortex clean / Machine clean quality
  • Uniform grain size and natural reddish-brown color
  • Low moisture and free from infestation

Finger Millet Flour

Standard or superfine mesh

Milled in hygienic facilities preserving the nutrient-dense outer bran layer. Available in fine mesh for bakery blends and standard mesh for traditional food preparations.

  • 100% pure finger millet with no additives
  • Custom mesh sizes available on request
  • Consistent texture and moisture stability

Common applications

Bakery & Confectionery

Used in high-fiber artisan breads, gluten-reduced cookies, biscuits, crackers, and health muffins.

Baby & Weaning Foods

A traditional staple for infant weaning cereals and porridge mixes due to high bioavailable calcium and ease of digestion.

Extruded & Ready-to-Eat Snacks

Base ingredient for millet flakes, breakfast cereals, healthy puffs, and instant health drinks.

Retail Repackaging

Supplied in bulk for consumer brands and private labels packaging raw ragi and packaged flour.
