Dehydrated Powders & Flakes

Processed from farm-fresh produce under hygienic, temperature-controlled conditions to preserve volatile essential oils, authentic taste, and vibrant natural color. We supply single ingredients and custom dehydrates in commercial volumes.

Product catalogue

Each product is available in chopped flakes, granules, or pulverized fine powders.

Onion (White & Red)

Flakes, minced, granules & fine powder

Prepared from selected white and red onion bulbs. Delivers intense allium aroma and instant solubility in gravies, sauces, and seasoning mixes.

  • Toasted or natural non-toasted options
  • Free from caking agents and impurities
  • Moisture controlled below 5%

Garlic

Flakes, chopped, granules & powder

Pungent, high-allicin garlic cloves dehydrated under low temperature to retain genuine flavor strength and pungent bite.

  • Crisp white to light cream color
  • Uniform particle sizing from coarse to 100 mesh
  • Rehydrates swiftly in hot liquid

Ginger (Dry Ginger Powder)

High gingerol & shogaol profile

Milled from peeled and dried ginger rhizomes. Clean, warm, peppery punch suitable for baked confectionery, chai blends, and marinades.

  • Natural light buff color
  • Low crude fiber and clean aroma
  • Ideal for nutraceuticals and seasoning blends

Turmeric Powder

High Curcumin varieties (3% – 5%+)

Sourced from prime turmeric growing belts. Deep golden-yellow hue, tested for heavy metal limits and zero synthetic dyes.

  • Standardized curcumin percentages
  • Fine ground for rapid dispersion
  • Clean aroma with warm earthy undertones

Tomato Powder

Spray dried & hot air dehydrated

Rich, tangy red tomato powder produced from ripe field tomatoes. Provides instant body, authentic acidity, and color to dry soup bases.

  • 100% natural, free from added MSG
  • Easily dissolvable in water or sauce bases
  • Natural lycopene content

Coriander, Curry Leaves & Mint

Herb powders & dehydrated leaves

Selected fresh culinary herbs dehydrated to lock in aromatic terpenes. Retains emerald-green tint and fresh garden bouquet.

  • Coriander powder & crushed seeds
  • Dehydrated whole curry leaves and curry leaf powder
  • Crisp dehydrated mint leaves and fine mint powder

Specifications & quality standards

Manufactured in HACCP and ISO compliant dehydration plants.

Dehydration Process Continuous belt / cabinet hot-air drying / spray drying
Mesh Size / Particle Size Flakes (5–12mm), Granules (20–40 mesh), Fine Powder (60–100 mesh)
Moisture Content Powders: 4.0% – 5.5% Max | Flakes: 6.0% Max
Rehydration Ratio 1 : 4 to 1 : 6 (varies by item)
Total Ash 5% Max (average)
Additives & Preservatives Nil (100% Pure Agricultural Dehydrates)
Bulk Packaging 15 kg / 20 kg / 25 kg corrugated carton boxes with double food-grade polyethylene inner liners
Shelf Life 12 to 24 months in sealed original packaging under cool, dry storage
